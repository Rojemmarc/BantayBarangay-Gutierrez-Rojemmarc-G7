Core Capabilities

Built for fast, accountable barangay response

Everything needed to report neighborhood issues clearly, protect resident privacy, and keep communities informed.

Photo & Map Pin Evidence

Attach up to 3 JPG/PNG photos with automatic EXIF metadata stripping and pin exact locations using Leaflet maps or device GPS.

Real-Time Status History

Every transition is logged with timestamps and official updates, sending in-app alerts to residents and assigned field staff.

Emergency Prioritization

Urgent reports are highlighted at the top of barangay queues and immediately trigger alerts for active barangay officials.

Privacy-First Reporting

Designed around Data Privacy Act (RA 10173) principles with barangay isolation and anonymous Safety reporting options.

Community Bulletin & Recognition

Officials publish advisories, events, and announcements while top civic reporters earn points on the barangay leaderboard.

Anonymized LGU Analytics

Local government units review cross-barangay aggregate totals and CSV exports without exposing resident personal data.

Workflow

How it works

A clear three-step process designed for both residents on mobile phones and barangay teams in the office.

1

Submit a concern

Log in, choose a category, describe the issue, attach evidence photos, and pin the location on the map.

2

Assign & coordinate

Barangay officials assign the right department and active staff member while volunteers share community updates.

3

Resolve & verify

Staff document the fix with comments and optional resolution proof photos, and residents review the full timeline.
